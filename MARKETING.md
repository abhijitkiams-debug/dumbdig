> **Note:** the game is now a **side-view artillery duel** (Pocket-Tanks style:

Launch-ready marketing copy for every surface. Everything here is written to the
platform's real character limits and tuned to the game's viral hooks: one-thumb
play, Pocket-Tanks aiming, roguelite boons, a daily shared battle, and the

good-vs-evil pull of the Ramayana.

## 10. Do / don't for the brand voice

**Do:** heroic, energetic, reverent of the source, confident, concise. Lean on
verbs — draw, aim, dodge, clash, unleash, sever.
**Don't:** mock or trivialize the Ramayana; over-promise multiplayer/online
features the game doesn't have; use graphic-violence language ("gore", "blood").
Keep it mythic and celebratory, not grim.
