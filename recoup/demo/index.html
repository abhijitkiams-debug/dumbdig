Recoup is a working prototype of the three-layer decision-support framework in Lappas & Xanthopoulos, “Intelligent decision support for debt collection using predictive learning and multi-criteria optimization,” Finance Research Open (2026). Layer 1 rule extraction (K-means segments) · Layer 2 prediction (Random Forest P2P/APP) · Layer 3 optimization (fuzzy priority, TOPSIS, AHP, routing). All figures come from a synthetic portfolio — no real debtor data is used.
